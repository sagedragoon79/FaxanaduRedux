# Pass 02: M2 combat core

Date: 2026-10-23. Scope: milestone M2, plus the follow-ups from pass 01.

## Input since pass 01

Sean played the M1 movement gym in Godot 4.7.2 on his PC: "feels good so far. Keep going."
That is the first hands-on check of the movement feel.

## What was built

- **Melee:** three-hit ground combo (8, 8, 14 damage; the third is a heavy hit with bigger
  hit-stop, shake and knockback), two-hit air combo, alternating smear arcs, a 0.15 s attack buffer.
- **Shield:** tap Block to parry (0.12 s window, staggers the attacker, restores MP), hold
  to guard (half damage for MP, slow walk).
- **Magic:** MP that regenerates and refills on melee hits; a spell registry with Deluge as
  the first spell (water bolt that hooks into future fire seals and basins).
- **Red Potion:** two charges, 0.7 s drink that leaves you rooted and is cancelled by a hit.
- **Enemies:** an `Enemy` base (hit flash, knockback, stagger, death, gold, respawn) and three
  enemies with telegraphs of 0.4 s or longer: Monodron (hopper), Hornet (swooping flier),
  Rot Dwarf (shielded walker with a parryable axe swing).
- **Gold and death:** coins that pop, bounce and magnet to you; on death 60% of your gold
  stays behind in a purse you can recover; dying again loses it.
- **Feel and presentation:** sub-pixel smooth camera (world rendered in a pixel viewport,
  shifted by the camera's fractional position), red damage flash, a real HUD (health masks,
  MP bar, potion charges, rolling gold counter), 13 new synthesized sounds.
- **Gym:** a combat arena with all three enemies (respawning after 5 s). The gym is now
  generated by `tools/levels/gen_gym.py`.

## Automated verification

52 tests, 95 checks, all passing, three consecutive runs. New: combo order and damage,
combo reset, air combo length, attack buffer, parry, guard, knockback, potion and
interruption, Deluge cost, hit and gating, MP on hit, gold collection, death purse and its
loss on a second death, contact damage, camera sub-pixel and bounds, each enemy's telegraph
and approach, the Rot Dwarf's shield sides, respawning, and a random-input soak (3 seeds x
900 frames in the gym: no escapes, no embedding in solid tiles, no invalid positions).
