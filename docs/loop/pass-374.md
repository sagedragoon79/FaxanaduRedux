# Pass 374: the fall you can hear (Audio)

Category: Audio (last pass 366).

## Audit

Passes 367 to 373 added three things that could make a sound:
- the drowned statue (pass 371) is still stone, so it should be silent;
- the minimap (pass 373) only draws, while the full map keeps its open and close sounds;
- the Drowned Aqueduct's broken channel (pass 371) pours a white fall into the flood, and it was silent.

Every other moving water in the game has a voice: the fountains' splash, the flood and the waves. The forge's anvil already shows the pattern for a set piece heard nearby, fading with distance. The mix check passed, and the sound list (13.3) counted 165 effects.

## Fix

- `falls_pour` (tools/audio/sfxgen.py) is a two-second loop. It layers a white hiss of falling water over a low churning roar that gulps at 1.5 Hz. The last half second is crossfaded over the first, so the loop has no hard seam. Its gain is 0.3, and `tools/audio/levels.py --check` passes. Every other sound effect regenerated by sfxgen was checked back out.
- `AqueductSpill` plays it through its own `AudioStreamPlayer` on the Sfx bus, looped forward:
  - it plays at 4 dB under the sound volume where the water lands (`LOUD`);
  - it fades 22 dB over 320 px (`HEAR`, `FADE`);
  - it is silent beyond that, and with no sound device;
  - `heard_db(at)` gives the level for a listener.
- Bible 13.3: 166 sound effects, and a note "The fall you can hear (pass 374)".
- `test_the_aqueducts_fall_is_heard_near_it` (test_rooms) checks:
  - the voice is on the sound bus;
  - the pour loops;
  - it is loudest where the water lands;
  - it fades with distance;
  - it is silent past its reach and at both doors;
  - the bible's note.

Mutation check: two changes, made together, failed 4 checks (the loop, the reach, and both doors):
- a reach ten times longer;
- no loop flag.

I dropped one seam check. It compared the loop's first and last samples, but the imported stream is compressed, and for broadband noise the jump from one sample to the next is no measure of a click. The loop's crossfade is the generator's work, and the bible says how it is made rather than claiming a measured seam.
