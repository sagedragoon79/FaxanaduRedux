# Pass 04: art cohesion

Date: 2026-10-23. Scope: the lowest category from pass 03 (art cohesion, 40). The Autosprite
tool is still not connected in this environment, so the player animation set is generated by
code instead; no credits were spent and the ledger is unchanged.

## What was built

- **Player sprite sheet** (`tools/art/spritegen.py`): a small 2D rig with two-bone IK, each
  part shaded with a top-left key light, separated by a darker line of its own ramp, and fully
  outlined, every pixel on the master palette. 16 animations: idle, run (8 frames), rise,
  apex, fall, dash, wall slide, three combo swings, up slash, down strike, block, drink,
  cast, hurt.
- **Animation playback** (`PlayerVisual`): picks the animation from the player's state, plays
  run at a rate tied to speed, restarts swings on every press, and lets a swing's recovery
  frames finish over idle, run or air poses. The blade, shield and potion are drawn from each
  frame's hand point (blade length grows with the weapon's reach). The scarf hangs from each
  frame's neck point, so it stays attached through dashes and lunges. Dash afterimages use the
  frame's silhouette.
- **Room decor** (`tools/art/decorgen.py`, `Decor`): floor tufts, cyan glowing fungus,
  mushrooms and twigs, hanging roots and moss, and wall shelf fungus in the Trunk; weeds,
  stones, crates, ivy, chains, banners and wall sconces in Eolis. Scattered from the room id,
  clear of doors and hazards. Light-giving pieces get an additive pulsing glow.
- **Particle layer** (`Motes`): spores rising in the Trunk, dust drifting in Eolis, at two
  depths of parallax, wrapping around the view.
- **Fix:** slow motion from the boss kill leaked into later tests (it runs on wall-clock time).
  `Feel.reset()` now runs before every test.

## Review against the bible

- **12.3 rendering rules:** full character outline, selective inner lines, 3 tones per
  material with hue-shifted shadows, no dithering on characters: met by the generator.
- **12.4 animation:** attacks have strike, follow-through and recovery. Deviation: the
  player's swings skip anticipation because the hitbox is live on the press; the bible now
  says so.
- **12.1 size:** the body is about 20x46 px (hitbox 12x40); with blade and scarf it fills
  the 48x56 envelope. The bible now says so.
- **12.5 layers:** far, mid, tiles, decor, particles. Still missing: foreground silhouettes
  and a third background layer.
- Not yet: enemies and NPCs are still the old rectangle placeholders, so the player now
  outclasses them on screen.
